Every day the model ranks the ~1,000 most-traded NASDAQ companies against each other for the next 5 trading days. It does not predict whether the market goes up; it predicts which stocks do better than the others. "Expected vs median" and "chance to beat the median" come from how each score decile actually did out of sample. The edge per stock is small (published systems: correlation 0.04–0.05); it only shows up across many stocks and weeks.
Price forecasts (v7, shadow): median and ranges for the next 5 trading days and the chance of being up. Ranges were well calibrated in testing (80% range held 80% of the time); "up or down" was right 51% of the time overall and 56–58% for the bottom-ranked stocks. Market direction itself is not predicted.
